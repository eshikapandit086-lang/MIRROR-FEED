MIRROR-FEED — Look Beyond the Feed

What if scrolling could become an investigation instead of a habit?

MIRROR-FEED is a youth media literacy concept inspired by UNESCO's Media and Information Literacy framework.

It explores how people can become more aware of the narratives, patterns and influences behind the content they consume on social media.

The Problem

Social media feeds are personalised, but users don't always stop to question why certain content appears repeatedly, how it influences their perceptions, or what narratives they are consuming.

MIRROR-FEED aims to encourage a more active and reflective relationship with digital content.

The Concept

Instead of treating a social media feed as something to consume passively, MIRROR-FEED approaches it as something users can examine and question.

Key Focus Areas

- Youth media literacy
- Critical thinking about digital content
- Awareness of social media narratives
- Reflection on content consumption
- Understanding the influence of personalised feeds

Inspiration

The project is rooted in the UNESCO Media and Information Literacy (MIL) framework.

Project Goal

To encourage users to question what they see online, recognise patterns in their digital environments and become more intentional media consumers.

Status

An experimental project exploring the intersection of digital wellbeing, media literacy and technology.
